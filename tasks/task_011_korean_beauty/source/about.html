Our Founder · 창립자

A dermatologist's quiet promise to her patients.

In 2019, after fifteen years of treating sensitive Korean skin at Yonsei Severance Hospital, Dr. Yuna Park began formulating in a small Seongsu-dong studio above a roastery. She was searching for what her patients couldn't find on shelves — gentle, evidence-based formulas that respected the skin barrier without sacrificing the soft sensoriality of K-beauty.

도담은 박유나 원장의 진료실에서 시작되었습니다. 자극 없이도 부드럽고 단단해질 수 있는 피부를 위해, 임상 데이터와 자연 성분을 가장 정직하게 담아냅니다.

Today, every Dodam formula is co-developed with the Kim & Park Clinical lab in Seoul and tested on 200+ volunteers across four skin types before release.

— Yuna Park, 박유나

Our Philosophy · 철학

We don't believe in overnight transformations. We believe in eight weeks of patient ritual.

느린 미용 — Slow Beauty

Founded by Seoul dermatology lab Kim & Park Clinical in 2019, Dodam is built on the principle that healthy skin is grown, not bought. Our formulas are designed for layering — the original Korean ritual — and every product is hypoallergenic-tested across all four skin types.

도담의 철학은 '느린 미용'에 있습니다. 빠른 변화보다는, 매일의 정성으로 가꾸는 피부. 우리는 그 여정에 가장 다정한 동반자가 되고 싶습니다.
