Our Philosophy · 브랜드 스토리

The art of slow beauty.

"느린 미용 — 피부는 천천히, 다정하게 길러야 합니다."

Founded in 2019 by Seoul dermatology lab Kim & Park Clinical, Dodam was born from a simple belief: that great skin can't be rushed. It's grown — gently, daily, over weeks and months.

Every formula begins in our Seongsu-dong studio and is hypoallergenic-tested on real Korean skin types before it ever reaches your shelf. We call it 느린 미용 — slow beauty. The ritual matters as much as the result.
