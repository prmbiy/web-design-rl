About Verisys

Founded 1997 in Reston, Virginia.  |  NASDAQ: VRSY  |  2,400+ employees worldwide
Our Mission
Empowering the Global Enterprise Verisys Enterprise Solutions exists to empower Fortune 1000 organizations through reliable, scalable software infrastructure that integrates mission-critical business processes across heterogeneous IT environments. We deliver measurable return on investment through proven J2EE technology, world-class professional services, and an unwavering commitment to customer success.
Company Timeline — A History of Innovation
Year Milestone
1997 Verisys Founded. Robert J. Chen and three former Oracle engineers incorporate Verisys Enterprise Solutions, Inc. in Reston, Virginia, with seed funding from Sequoia Capital and NEA.
1998 VeriSuite 1.0 Released. First commercial release of the VeriSuite ERP platform. Signs first Fortune 500 customer, Prudential Financial.
1999 IPO on NASDAQ. Verisys completes initial public offering on October 12, 1999 under ticker symbol VRSY. Opens European headquarters in Reading, United Kingdom.
2000 VeriCore J2EE Platform. Launches industry-first J2EE-native application framework. Achieves 187% year-over-year revenue growth.
2001 Acquired DataLogix Corp. Completes $284M cash-and-stock acquisition of DataLogix Corp., adding data warehousing capabilities now branded as VeriVault.
2002 APAC Expansion. Opens regional offices in Tokyo, Singapore, and Sydney. Achieves ISO 9001:2000 certification across all engineering operations.
2003 Launched VeriSuite 7.2. Latest release delivers Sarbanes-Oxley compliance reporting, enhanced .NET interoperability, and 99.999% uptime SLA. Named to Gartner Leaders Quadrant.
