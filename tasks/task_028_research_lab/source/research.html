Research Areas

Four interconnected threads on language, reasoning, and trustworthy AI — building language models that reason, explain, and remain robust.

Thread 01 · Foundations

Large Language Models

We study the architectural and algorithmic foundations of large autoregressive language models — with a particular focus on how sparse computation, attention routing, and chain-structured decoding affect a model's ability to perform faithful multi-step reasoning. Our work spans pre-training experiments at the 1B–13B scale and post-hoc analyses of frontier systems.

Thread 02 · Grounding

Multimodal Learning

We develop vision-language systems that are pixel-faithful: capable of grounding linguistic claims to specific image regions, video frames, or temporal scene segments. Our approach combines dual-encoder pretraining, region-level contrastive objectives, and temporal scene graph supervision.

Thread 03 · Safety

Robustness

We study the failure modes of deployed language models under adversarial prompts, distribution shift, and prompt-injection. Our work develops both attacks — to surface real risk — and defenses, including instruction-tuned safety adapters and runtime monitors that detect jailbreak attempts before generation completes.

Thread 04 · Explanation

Interpretability

We reverse-engineer the computational mechanisms inside trained transformers — identifying attention heads, MLP neurons, and residual-stream circuits that implement specific behaviors. Recent work isolates the circuits responsible for refusal in instruction-tuned 7B models and traces how those circuits shift under fine-tuning.

Ongoing Projects

Currently-active artifacts and benchmarks released by the lab.

BENCHMARK

REASON-Bench

A 12,400-question stress-test suite for multi-hop reasoning, covering arithmetic, planning, and counterfactual chains. Released under MIT license.

MODEL + DATA

OpenGroundVL

An open recipe for region-grounded vision-language models trained on 18M image-region-caption triples. Checkpoints at 1.3B and 7B scales.

EVALUATION

SAFRON-Eval

DARPA SAFRON-funded evaluation harness for adversarial robustness of frontier LMs, with automated red-team probes and reproducible scorecards.

LIBRARY

Yale Interpretability Toolkit

A Python library for causal patching, attribution, and circuit discovery on HuggingFace transformer models. v0.4 released October 2023.
