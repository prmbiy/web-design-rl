Building language models that reason, explain, and remain robust.

Research Focus

Mission

YNRL studies the foundations of language understanding in large neural models. Our work concentrates on three interlocking questions: how to make model reasoning faithful to its underlying computation, how to ground language in perception through multimodal learning, and how to harden models against adversarial inputs and distributional shift. We publish open code, benchmarks, and datasets, and we collaborate broadly with the academic and industrial NLP community.
