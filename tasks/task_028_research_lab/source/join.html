How to Apply (PhD)

YNRL admits PhD students through the Yale Department of Computer Science. There is no separate lab application — please apply to the Yale CS PhD program and indicate Prof. Aditi Ramaswamy as a faculty of interest in your statement of purpose.

Application deadline: December 15, 2023 (11:59pm ET) for Fall 2024 entry. Apply via the Yale Graduate School application portal.

What We Look For

  • Strong mathematical and machine-learning foundations (linear algebra, probability, optimization).
  • Systems depth (CUDA, distributed training, large-scale data pipelines) or linguistic depth (formal semantics, syntax, pragmatics).
  • Prior research output: a workshop paper, conference submission, open-source release, or strong undergraduate thesis.
  • A clear articulation of a research question you would want to spend five years on.
  • Comfort with collaborative, reading-group-driven scholarship.

Note: we read every statement of purpose carefully. Please do not use generic templates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the GRE required?
No. Yale CS has made the GRE optional indefinitely. Scores submitted will not be held against an applicant, but they are not necessary.
What is the funding situation?
Admitted PhD students receive a five-year guarantee of full tuition, stipend, and health insurance through a combination of fellowships, RA, and TA appointments.
Should I email Prof. Ramaswamy before applying?
A brief, specific email referencing one of our recent papers is welcome but not required. Generic "I'm interested in your lab" emails will not receive a reply due to volume.
What is the lab culture like?
Weekly reading group, biweekly 1:1s with the PI, paper-writing collaboration across subgroups, low hierarchy. We expect students to develop independent agendas by year two.
Are summer internships allowed?
Yes, starting after the second year. We encourage internships at industry labs (Google, Anthropic, Microsoft Research, Allen AI) when the project complements the student's thesis.
Can I work remotely?
PhD students are expected to be resident in New Haven during the academic year. Short remote stints (conferences, family, collaborations) are normal. Postdocs and visiting researchers may negotiate hybrid arrangements.

Visiting Researchers

Stays of 3–12 months

YNRL hosts a small number of senior PhD students (third year and beyond) and faculty on sabbatical each year. Visitors are embedded in an active project, attend lab meetings, and typically co-author at least one paper during their stay.

We provide desk space at AKW Hall, library and compute access, and informal mentorship from the PI. Visitors must arrange their own funding (institutional sabbatical support, Fulbright, JSPS, etc.) and visa sponsorship through Yale OISS.

Inquiries: ynrl-visiting@cs.yale.edu — please attach a CV and a one-page research proposal. Read about current visitors →
