Prof. Aditi Ramaswamy

Associate Professor of Computer Science · Yale University

Aditi directs YNRL, with research spanning faithful reasoning in large language models, mechanistic interpretability of transformer circuits, and the design of trustworthy evaluation protocols. Prior to Yale, she was a research scientist at Google Brain and earned her PhD from Stanford in 2017.

Interests: reasoning, interpretability, robust evaluation, multimodal grounding.

Featured paper: Chain-of-Verification Decoding (NeurIPS 2023) → aditi.cs.yale.edu Google Scholar ramaswamy@cs.yale.edu

PhD Spotlight

Featured this quarter
HP
★ Spotlight

Hana Park · 4th-year PhD

Hana's recent work isolates the narrow set of attention heads that mediate refusal behavior in instruction-tuned 7B language models, opening a path toward editable safety mechanisms. Her ICLR 2024 paper extends this analysis across model scales.

"What surprised us is that 'refusal' isn't a diffuse property — it lives in a handful of heads. That's good news, and slightly terrifying news, all at once."

→ Scaling Refusal Circuits: A Cross-Model Study · ICLR 2024 · arXiv:2401.10882 · code
