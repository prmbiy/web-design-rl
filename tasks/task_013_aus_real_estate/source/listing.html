Renovated Edwardian charm in coveted Auburn High zone

Behind a classic tessellated tile veranda lies a beautifully renovated four-bedroom Edwardian residence, blending period elegance with thoughtful contemporary updates. Polished Tasmanian oak floorboards, soaring 3.3m ceilings, leadlight windows and ornate cornices run through the formal lounge and central hallway.

A light-filled open-plan kitchen, meals and family room opens onto the deep north-facing rear garden — perfect for entertaining year-round. Stone benchtops, integrated Miele appliances and a butler's pantry complete the chef's kitchen.

Four generous bedrooms include a sumptuous main with walk-in robe and ensuite. Zoning to Auburn High and walking distance to Glenferrie Road shops, cafés and Hawthorn Station make this an exceptional family opportunity.

  • Ducted heating & refrigerated cooling
  • North-facing rear garden
  • Auto double garage with rear ROW
  • Hydronic heating in bathrooms
  • Auburn High & Hawthorn West PS zones
  • Solar panels (6.6kW system)
  • Cellar / wine storage
