Why buyers are heading north of Brunswick

Once a quiet pocket of postwar bungalows, Coburg has become one of Melbourne's most-watched suburbs. Sydney Road's Lebanese bakeries and Persian grocers sit beside specialty coffee, and the Upfield bike path delivers a stress-free commute to the CBD. Families are drawn to Coburg High's rebuild and the parklands of Merri Creek.
