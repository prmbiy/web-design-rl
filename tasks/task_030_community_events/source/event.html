Sunset Rooftop Vinyl Night — Jazz & Soul Edition

Sat, Oct 12 • 6:00–10:00 PM • Westlight, Williamsburg

SAT · OCT 12 · 6PM 📍 Westlight Rooftop, Williamsburg
W&
Wax & Wine Co. Verified · 12.4k followers

About this event

Watch the Manhattan skyline melt into gold from the top of Westlight as Marcus Ellis and Lina Park spin a four-hour journey through deep jazz, dusty soul 45s, and rare grooves — all from vinyl, all night. Sip from a curated natural wine bar, settle into floor cushions in the listening lounge, and enter the raffle for a hand-picked record from our crate. This is slow listening, golden hour, and the city humming below.

Capacity is intentionally small. Doors at 6PM sharp — late entry isn't guaranteed.
