Frequently Asked

Good questions, answered.

The fine print, plain spoken.

How do I cancel my membership?
Monthly memberships can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice — just email community@kilnandco.com or stop by the front desk. Annual members can cancel and we'll prorate the remaining months at the monthly rate.
Can I bring guests, and what's the policy?
Yes — guest passes vary by tier (2/4/8 per month for Hot Desk, Dedicated, and Private Office respectively). Sign guests in at the front desk. Guests have coffee bar and wifi access but cannot book meeting rooms independently.
Is after-hours access really 24/7?
For Dedicated Desk and Private Office members, yes — your keyfob works around the clock, every day of the year, at all three Brooklyn locations. The coffee bar is staffed weekdays 8am–7pm; after hours, the self-serve beer tap and snack pantry stay open.
Can I add teammates to my Private Office?
Absolutely. Private Office pricing scales with team size — pricing starts at $899/mo for a one-person studio and goes up to our Team Suite at $2,499/mo+ for 8–12 person teams. Each teammate gets full member benefits.
Can I pause my membership if I'm traveling?
Yes. Members in good standing can pause their membership for up to 60 days per calendar year at $49/mo, which holds your seat or office and keeps your community access (Slack, events, partner perks) intact.
