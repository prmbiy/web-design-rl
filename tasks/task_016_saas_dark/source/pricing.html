Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Email support@flexor.dev.

What counts as a Monthly Active User (MAU)?
An MAU is any unique user identifier (typically your internal user ID) that Flexor receives in an evaluate() call within a rolling 30-day window. Anonymous visitors with a stable device ID count too. We deduplicate aggressively, so a single user across 10 sessions is still one MAU.
Can I self-host Flexor?
Self-hosting is available exclusively on the Enterprise plan, including our Self-Hosted Relay for keeping all evaluation traffic inside your VPC. Pro and Team plans run on our SOC 2 Type II–certified multi-tenant cloud.
Do you offer startup discounts?
Yes. Pre-seed and seed-stage startups (under $5M raised) get 50% off Pro and Team plans for 12 months. Y Combinator, Techstars, and a16z portfolio companies receive an additional 6 months free — just email startups@flexor.dev with your AngelList or Crunchbase link.
Is there a free trial of Pro?
Every paid plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. At the end of the trial, your workspace downgrades to Free automatically — nothing breaks, but premium features pause until you upgrade.
What happens if I exceed my MAU limit?
On Pro and Team, we never block evaluations. We bill overage at $0.50 per additional 10,000 MAU at the end of the month. On Free, evaluations beyond 50k MAU return your configured fallback value and we send a friendly email asking you to upgrade.
Which SDKs do you support?
First-party SDKs for Node.js, TypeScript, Python, Go, Rust, Ruby, Java, .NET, PHP, Swift, Kotlin, React, React Native, Vue, and Flutter. Plus an Edge SDK for Cloudflare Workers, Vercel Edge, and Deno Deploy. All SDKs are open source on GitHub.
Can I switch from monthly to annual mid-cycle?
Yes — switching to annual is prorated instantly and the 20% discount applies to the remaining term. Switching from annual back to monthly takes effect at your renewal date.
How does data residency work?
Free, Pro, and Team plans run on AWS us-east-1 with replication to eu-west-1. Enterprise customers can choose dedicated EU (Frankfurt), APAC (Singapore), or US-only deployments with full data residency guarantees and DPA addendums.
