// Our Story — Est. 2022 Taipei

We built this in a garage
because no one else would.

From a sensor board prototype in a Taipei garage to 4,812 backers in 38 countries. This is how Field One happened.

MC
A letter from the founder
Mei Lin Chen
CEO & Co-founder · Ex-RED Digital Cinema

Why $4K should not be the floor for cinema.

I spent six years at RED watching brilliant filmmakers walk into our showroom, hold a camera that could change their work, then walk back out because the entry point started at $4,995 — body only. That number is a moat. It's not engineering. It's not silicon cost. It's a moat.

In 2021 I started sketching a different kind of camera on weekends. Something a working DP could afford on a single shoot. Something a film student could pre-order without taking on debt. Open-gate Super 35. Internal RAW. A real cinema mount. The kind of feature list that engineers at every major brand have whiteboarded a hundred times and been told "no, that's a $9,000 camera."

"The hardest part of building Field One was not the sensor. It was convincing twelve suppliers that yes, we really did mean $899."

We started in a garage in Wenshan District in March 2022. The first sensor board didn't even hit a frame buffer — it just smoked. The second one did 2K and crashed every 90 seconds. By prototype seven we had clean 6K 30p. By prototype eleven we had a body you could hold.

Then we did the thing every hardware founder is warned against: we launched on Kickstarter without a brand, without press, without a partner. We hit our $400K goal in 38 minutes. We hit $1.6M in 11 days. 4,812 people pre-ordered a camera from a company that had never shipped a camera.

That trust is the only reason this product exists. We owe those backers — and now you — a camera that is honest about what it is and what it isn't. No marketing-speak in our spec sheet. No asterisks on our frame rates. Every number on this site is measured, not estimated.

Thank you for reading this far. Now go shoot something.

Mei Lin Chen
CEO, NOVA · Taipei, October 2024
// Journey — Prototype to Product

Two and a half years.
Eleven prototypes.

Mar 2022

First sensor board in a Taipei garage

Prototype 01 boots, captures one frame at 1080p, then the regulator dies. Mei Lin and Daniel celebrate with cold dumplings. Total budget spent: NT$184,000.

P01
Nov 2022

NovaCore X2 silicon taped out

Custom image processor design returns from fab in Hsinchu. Yuki ports the firmware over a 72-hour bench session. First clean 4K capture follows two weeks later.

P04
Jul 2023

EVT body — the chassis becomes real

CNC machining of the magnesium top plate begins with a partner shop in Taoyuan. The camera weighs 638g. We commit to 612g for shipping units.

P07
Sep 2023

Kickstarter hits $1.6M in 11 days

Campaign goes live with one product video shot on prototype 09. Funded in 38 minutes. 412% by closing. 4,812 backers across 38 countries.

KS
Apr 2024

PVT — pilot production runs in Hsinchu

200 units assembled on the SMT line. Calibration bench commissioned. We discover and fix a sensor stitching bug at iso 1600 that delays shipping by six weeks. Worth it.

P10
Aug 2024

DVT units ship to 40 reviewers worldwide

Embargo set for January 15, 2025. Production tooling locked. Field One enters mass production prep. Backer #001 unit is engraved and set aside.
