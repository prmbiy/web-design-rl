// The verdict

Field One wins on weight, price, and mount flexibility.

The FX3 has the edge on log latitude. The Pocket 6K G2 matches us on internal RAW. But neither is half the weight, swaps mounts in under eight seconds, or comes in under $900. For run-and-gun cinema, the math is one-sided.
