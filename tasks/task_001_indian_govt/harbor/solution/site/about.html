HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & TIMELINE

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj and Rural Empowerment was carved out of the erstwhile Ministry of Rural Development on 27th May 2004 as a separate Ministry of the Government of India to provide focused attention to the constitutional mandate enshrined in Part IX of the Constitution of India for the establishment and functioning of Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs). The Ministry is responsible for monitoring the implementation of constitutional provisions relating to the Panchayats and various Centrally Sponsored Schemes for strengthening rural local self-government.

YearMilestone
1957Balwantrai Mehta Committee recommended a three-tier Panchayati Raj structure (Gram Panchayat, Panchayat Samiti, Zilla Parishad).
1977Ashok Mehta Committee recommended two-tier system and constitutional status for Panchayats.
199273rd Constitutional Amendment Act passed by Parliament; came into force on 24th April 1993, providing constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions.
2004Ministry of Panchayati Raj constituted on 27th May 2004 as a separate Ministry under the Government of India.
2010National Panchayati Raj Day declared on 24th April; first observed at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi.
2012e-Panchayat Mission Mode Project launched under National e-Governance Plan (NeGP).
2014Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana (SAGY) launched on 11th October 2014, the birth anniversary of Loknayak Jayaprakash Narayan.
2018Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA) restructured and launched on 24th April 2018; Digital Panchayat initiative rolled out across 2.5 Lakh Gram Panchayats.

VISION & MISSION

VISION To attain decentralized and participatory local self-government through empowered, accountable and transparent Panchayats that emerge as vibrant institutions of grassroots democracy, planning, social justice and inclusive economic development of villages and rural communities across Bharat.
MISSION (i) Capacity building of elected representatives and functionaries of Panchayats; (ii) Effective devolution of functions, finances and functionaries (3Fs) to PRIs by States/UTs; (iii) Mainstreaming e-governance through e-Gram Swaraj for transparent planning, accounting and monitoring; (iv) Promoting social audit, citizen engagement and Gram Sabha empowerment; (v) Strengthening the role of Panchayats in attainment of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) at local level.
OBJECTIVES Implementation of Part IX of the Constitution; administration of Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act 1996 (PESA); monitoring of State Finance Commissions; coordination of Centrally Sponsored Schemes for rural local bodies.

ALLOCATION OF BUSINESS RULES, 1961

Sl.Subject Allocated to the Ministry
1.All matters relating to the Panchayati Raj Institutions and the implementation of the provisions contained in Part IX of the Constitution of India.
2.Administration of the Provisions of the Panchayats (Extension to the Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996 (40 of 1996) (PESA Act).
3.Co-ordination with the State Governments and Union Territory Administrations regarding all matters relating to Panchayati Raj.
4.Monitoring and review of the constitution and functioning of State Finance Commissions and devolution of powers to PRIs.
5.Centrally Sponsored Schemes: Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA), Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana (SAGY), Incentivisation of Panchayats.
6.Capacity building, training and e-Governance initiatives for elected representatives and functionaries of Panchayats.
7.Administration of National Institute of Rural Development and Panchayati Raj (NIRDPR), Hyderabad and its regional centres.
8.All matters relating to the National Panchayati Raj Day (24th April) and conferment of Panchayat Sashaktikaran Puraskar.
9.Liaison with the Finance Commission of India regarding grants-in-aid to Panchayats.
10.Implementation of e-Panchayat Mission Mode Project under the National e-Governance Plan (NeGP).
