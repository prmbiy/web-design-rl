Cancellation Policy

House Rules, Quietly Kept

  • Cancellation Reservations may be modified or cancelled up to 48 hours prior to your seating at no charge.
  • No-Shows A $50 per seat fee applies to no-shows at Otsu by Verleux. Other restaurants charge $25 per seat after the first occurrence.
  • Card Hold A valid credit card is required to secure all reservations. No charge is placed unless cancellation terms are not met.
  • Late Arrivals After a 15-minute grace period, tables may be released. Please call ahead if delayed.
