01 / THE BRIEF

Koruna Hodinářství — founded 1929 in Nové Město — had survived four regimes, two currencies, and one global pandemic. What they could not survive was indifference. The fourth-generation owners came to us with a question disguised as a brief: How do you make a mechanical watch matter to people who measure time on a screen they can't put down?

We refused the obvious answers. No "heritage reframed." No softened serifs. The work we delivered — identity, e-commerce platform, packaging system, a film — is built on a single insistence: a watch made by hand for ninety-four years deserves a brand that screams instead of whispers.
