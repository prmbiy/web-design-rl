↘ Manifesto Five non-negotiables 2017 — Present
N°01
Craft is the only currency. Everything else depreciates.
N°02
We argue. We bring conflict into the room because politeness produces beige work.
N°03
No templates. No moodboards from last quarter. No design that looks like everyone else's design.
N°04
We build for the long now. Identities that last twenty years, not twenty months.
N°05
Generative AI does not make brands. Humans with opinions do.
