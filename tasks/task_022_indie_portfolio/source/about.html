About Kai

Senior engineer at Linear. Based in Lisbon. Previously Vercel, Stripe, and a failed startup you've never heard of.

K.W

photo_and_bio.md

I grew up in Osaka in the late 90s, building HTML 4 fan sites for video games my friends didn't play. My parents thought computers were a phase. They were sort of right — I burned out, studied literature for four years, and ended up writing translations for an architecture magazine before remembering that I actually liked code more than I liked metaphor.

In 2016 I did a six-week bootcamp in Berlin and somehow talked my way into a contract role at Stripe a year later. Five years and two teams later I left to join Vercel during the great DX gold rush, where I built developer tools for a living and remembered why I started in the first place. I'm currently at Linear working on the sync engine — the thing that makes the app feel like magic when it isn't.

I moved to Portugal in 2022 for the same reasons everyone else did: the light, the food, and the rent. I work from a small apartment in Príncipe Real with a view of two churches and a tiled wall. I don't intend to leave.
