A gentle reminder

On cancellations.

We hold a twelve-hour cancellation window so that another practitioner may take your space. Cancellations made within twelve hours of class will count against your class pack or be charged at the drop-in rate. We understand life moves in ways we cannot predict — please write to us if something unexpected arrives.
