The Practice

Three ways to arrive.

Signature formats designed for the body you brought today — nothing more, nothing less.

Slow Flow Vinyasa

An unhurried sequence linking breath to movement. We pause where most studios rush, and we rest before we need to.

Teacher Spotlight · This Month

Amara Okafor on returning to the breath.

"The breath is the one teacher who never stopped showing up for you. Our practice is simply remembering how to listen."

Amara has taught at Stillwater since 2019. Her morning flows draw from a decade of Ashtanga study in Mysore and a softer, somatic approach she developed through doula work in Brooklyn.
