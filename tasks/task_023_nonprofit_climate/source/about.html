Our Mission

Three pillars. One deadline.

Climate Horizon Collective exists to compress the gap between climate science and political reality — by funding the people, evidence, and stories that move policy.

PILLAR 01

Rigorous science communication

We translate IPCC findings and peer-reviewed research into accessible briefings used by journalists, legislators, and 240,000 weekly subscribers to The Horizon Brief.

PILLAR 02

Frontline community support

We direct unrestricted funding, legal aid, and organizing capacity to communities on the receiving end of fossil-fuel infrastructure and climate disasters.

PILLAR 03

Structural policy change

We coordinate lobbying, litigation, and electoral programs targeting the federal, state, and corporate levers that determine whether emissions actually fall.

Founder of the modern environmental justice movement. Distinguished Professor at Texas Southern University; advisor to the EPA on Justice40.

FO

Attribution Science · Imperial College

Co-lead of World Weather Attribution. Pioneered the rapid attribution methodology now used to link heatwaves and floods to fossil emissions.

Earth Systems · PIK Potsdam

Director of the Potsdam Institute. Lead author on planetary boundaries framework and tipping-point cascades research.

Co-founder of Urban Ocean Lab, co-editor of "All We Can Save." Advisor on coastal resilience and blue-carbon policy.
