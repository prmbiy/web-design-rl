Where we're fighting

Three campaigns shaping the next 24 months.

Each campaign pairs frontline organizing with policy levers and rigorous science.

Energy

Coal-Free Grid by 2030

Accelerating retirement of the remaining 173 U.S. coal units through utility commission filings, community benefit agreements, and just-transition funds.

62 units retired since 2022→
Methane

Methane Watch: Permian Basin

Satellite-and-ground monitoring of super-emitter events across the largest U.S. oil field, with public dashboards used by EPA enforcement.

2,140 leaks documented→
Youth Power

Youth Climate Lobby Day 2025

Training 800 young people from 50 states to meet their congressional offices in person — a coordinated week of pressure on appropriations.

March 18–21, Washington D.C.→
