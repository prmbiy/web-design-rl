Get urgent help for mental health

If you or someone you know is in a mental health crisis, help is available right now — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

  • Call Samaritans free on 116 123 — open 24/7
  • Text SHOUT to 85258 — free, anonymous text support
  • Call 111 and select the mental health option
  • If life is at risk, call 999 or go to A&E

Get help with NHS Talking Therapies

NHS Talking Therapies (formerly IAPT) offers free, confidential talking therapies on the NHS, including cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), counselling and guided self-help.

You don't need a referral from your GP — you can refer yourself directly.

  • Available to anyone aged 18 or over registered with a GP in England
  • Help for depression, anxiety, panic, phobias, PTSD and more
  • Sessions available in person, by phone or online

Support for carers

Caring for someone with a mental health condition can be rewarding, but it can also be hard work. Looking after your own wellbeing is important.

Tips for caring for yourself while caring for others:

  • Take regular breaks — even short ones can help
  • Stay connected with friends and family
  • Keep up with hobbies and activities you enjoy
  • Talk to your GP if you're struggling with low mood
  • Apply for a free carer's assessment from your local council
