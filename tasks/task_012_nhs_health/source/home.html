In an emergency

If you or someone else needs urgent help, call the right number for the right service.

999

Life-threatening emergencies. Call for severe bleeding, chest pain, loss of consciousness, severe allergic reactions or stroke symptoms.

111

Urgent but not life-threatening. Call or go to 111 online when you need help fast but it's not a 999 emergency.
