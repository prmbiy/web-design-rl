The "V8 Roadster" Build — Starting Fresh

Alright fellas, after lurking for 4 years and helping build a half dozen Miatas for friends, it's finally time to start MY build thread. Picked up this '94 R-Package with a tired but running 1.8 last weekend for $3,400. Body is mostly straight, frame rails are solid (checked with a magnet), and the soft top isn't completely shredded.

The plan: LS3 6.2L mated to a T56 Magnum, custom subframe from V8Roadsters, Wilwood big brakes, and FM Fox coilovers. Goal weight is 2,650 lbs with a 50/50 distribution. This thing is going to be a 400whp track weapon.

^ Day 1: home from the previous owner. Already love it.

Updates will be at least weekly. Questions, suggestions, and "you're crazy" comments all welcome. Let's do this. 🏁

Subscribed. Brad, you have NO idea how long the community has been waiting for someone to do a proper documented LS swap thread instead of a 4-page tease and disappearing act.

A few notes from my experience helping with two of these:

  • Trans tunnel — even with V8R kit, plan on at least minor sheet metal massaging if you want headers tucked tight
  • Cooling: do not cheap out. C&R or Ron Davis. Don't ask me how I know.
  • Hood clearance with LS3 intake is tight. If you go with FAST 102, expect a bulge or cowl hood.

Stickied this for now. Keep us posted. 👍

@NewGuy2012 — perfectly valid question. Short version: a properly executed LS swap actually adds only about 60–80 lbs over a stock NA. The all-aluminum LS-series engines weigh roughly the same as a built BP plus all its turbo plumbing, intercooler, manifold, and supporting bracing.
